Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Data Controller and Scope of this Policy

The Mohamena application is operated by Mishal Eid Al-Baqmi Marketing Services Company, which is the entity responsible for processing the personal data described in this policy. This policy explains the data we handle, the purposes for which it is used and shared, and your rights in relation to it when you use the application and its services.

For privacy-related inquiries or to exercise your rights, contact us at: info@mohamena.com.

2. Data We Collect and Its Sources

We collect the data you provide when registering, editing your profile, submitting a request, or contacting us, depending on your account type and the service you use. This includes:

  • Account and contact data: name, email address, mobile number, city, user role, preferred language, and login credentials.
  • Service provider data: company or office name, license number, classification, description, contact link, the photo or logo you choose to add to the profile, membership details, and account approval status.
  • Request data: service type, request description, request number and status, as well as employment or training request data you submit through the available services.
  • Correspondence, support, and notification data related to your account and requests, as well as subscription or payment request status necessary for managing the service, without collecting bank card details.

The technical services used to operate the application may process connection and operational data necessary to provide and secure the service, such as IP address, device information, error logs, and notification tokens, depending on the features used and your device settings.

3. Sign-In Through Third-Party Services

If you choose to register or sign in using Google, or Apple when this feature is available, we receive the identification data needed to create your account or sign you in, such as your account identifier, name, and the email address made available by the sign-in provider with your authorization. We do not receive the password for your Google or Apple account. The email address provided by Apple may be a relay address if you choose to hide your email.

You can manage the application's connection through your account settings with the relevant sign-in provider. Disconnecting the application there does not by itself delete your Mohamena account; to delete your account, follow the steps explained below.

4. Purposes for Which We Use Data

We use data to create and manage accounts; verify service provider information and approve their profiles; receive and manage requests and make their details available to the appropriate parties; process employment and training requests; manage memberships and payment request statuses; send service notifications; provide support; protect and troubleshoot the application; and improve the user experience.

Processing is carried out in accordance with the applicable legal basis, including providing the requested service and fulfilling related obligations, complying with legal requirements, and obtaining your consent when such processing requires it. Merely using the application does not constitute separate consent for any processing that requires explicit consent.

5. Who Can Access the Data?

  • Application administration: may access data to the extent necessary to manage accounts and requests, provide support, and review service providers.
  • Service providers: may view the request details made available to them in order to provide the service. The requester's name, mobile number, and email address are not displayed to them within the request details.
  • Users of the company and office directory: may view the profile information displayed about an establishment, without the account owner's personal data being made available merely by clicking the company name.
  • Technology and operations providers: we use technical services to operate the application and its database, including Adalo, as well as sign-in, hosting, notification, and email services when used. Data is shared only to the extent necessary to provide the relevant function, and we require entities processing data on our behalf to provide protection no less than that described in this policy and not to use the data outside the authorized purposes.
  • Competent authorities: we may disclose data when required by law or pursuant to a binding request from a competent authority, to the extent required.

We do not sell users' personal data to third parties.

6. Request Content and Attachments

The application does not allow images or documents to be attached to service requests. This is separate from the photo or logo that you may add to your profile.

Please do not include the names of parties, identification numbers, contact details, or unnecessary confidential information in the request description. Information entered in the description may be visible to the service provider as part of the request details, even if your personal account fields are not displayed.

7. Payments and External Links

Payments are made outside the application through a link sent by the company. The Mohamena application does not receive or store bank card numbers or card verification codes. Data you enter on the payment page is subject to the payment provider's privacy policy, and we may use payment status or a transaction reference to manage your subscription or request.

When you proceed to an external service or website, please review its privacy policy before entering your data.

8. Data Protection and Processing by Service Providers

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, use, modification, or disclosure, and we restrict access according to permissions and work-related need. However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Operating services may require data to be processed outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any such transfer is subject to the applicable legal requirements and safeguards for the protection of personal data. You may contact us to inquire about our data processors, processing locations, and the safeguards relating to your data.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account, profile, and request data for as long as necessary to operate your account and provide the services you requested. Support correspondence is retained to the extent necessary to address the relevant matter. When the purpose ends or the account is deleted, we delete the data associated with it unless applicable law requires certain data to be retained.

If certain records must be retained, such as financial records subject to a legal retention obligation, retention is limited to the data, purpose, and period required by law, after which the data is deleted when the retention requirement ends. You may request information about data exempted from deletion, the reason for its retention, and the retention period by contacting the privacy email address.

10. Deleting Your Account from Inside or Outside the Application

From inside the application: open the page for editing your personal information, tap "Delete Account," and then confirm deletion on the confirmation page. This option is available to both beneficiaries and service providers and includes deletion of the account, profile, requests, and related data, subject to the legal exceptions described above.

From outside the application: send a request to info@mohamena.com with the subject "Mohamena Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with the account. We may request limited information to verify that you own the account before completing the deletion. Do not send your password or bank card details.

Removing the application from your device or signing out does not delete your account. Deleting your account does not by itself constitute a request for a refund of paid amounts; payment matters are handled in accordance with the applicable subscription policy.

11. Your Rights and Choices

In accordance with applicable laws and regulations, you have the right to know how your data is processed, request access to and obtain a copy of it, correct or update it, request its destruction, and withdraw consent for processing based on consent without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn. Deleting necessary data or withdrawing consent may make it impossible to provide the related service.

You can modify the information available through your profile or send your request to the privacy email address. We handle rights requests within the legally prescribed timeframes after appropriate identity verification and explain any applicable exception. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent authority for personal data protection in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

12. Notifications, Marketing, and Policy Updates

We send notifications related to accounts, requests, and services, and you can control device notifications through your device settings. Marketing communications are sent only in accordance with applicable legal requirements and any required consent, and you may request that they stop by contacting us through the email address provided.

When our data practices or services change, we publish an updated version of this policy with its update date. We explain material changes and request renewed consent when required by law.
